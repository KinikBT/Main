About the Venture

Translating behavioural science into practical daily support.

Kinik Behavioural Tech is an early-stage Singapore research and product venture. We collaborate with registered counsellors, psychologists, and executive coaches to design ethical, consent-driven tools that reinforce healthy communication habits outside the consultation room.

Evidence-Based Frameworks

Grounded in established clinical and coaching science, including Cognitive Behavioural Therapy (CBT), Emotionally Focused Therapy (EFT), and interpersonal communication research.

Privacy-by-Design & PDPA Compliance

Engineered from day one around strict Singapore Personal Data Protection Act (PDPA) alignment, ephemeral processing, and zero invasive audio retention.

Human-Centered Wellness

Positioned strictly as a non-medical behavioural wellness tool designed to complement—never replace—licensed counsellors, mediators, and executive coaches.

01 · Individual

Individual Self-Regulation

Supporting self-aware professionals, parents, and clients in recognizing stress-induced communication habits and building calmer, more intentional responses.

02 · Interpersonal

Couples & Family Alignment

Helping mutually consenting partners practice constructive de-escalation and repair habits between counseling or mediation sessions.

03 · Clinical Validation

Practitioner Collaboration

Partnering with licensed therapists, dispute mediators, and leadership coaches in Singapore to co-design ethical, low-friction behavioural frameworks.
